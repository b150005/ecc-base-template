# Mandatory Disclaimer Block

This file holds the disclaimer text that **every** compliance-checklist
output must include verbatim. The Skill's output template hardcodes
this block at the top of the report; the Skill cannot remove or
shorten it.

The block ships in **English and Japanese**, in that order, because
the template's audience is bilingual and the underlying legal regimes
include both English-source (GDPR, CCPA, App Store, Google Play) and
Japanese-source (電気通信事業法, 特定商取引法, 個人情報保護法, 資金
決済法) primary materials.

## English block (verbatim)

```markdown
> **This output is not legal advice.** It is a structural checklist
> generated by an automated agent that surfaces legal regimes which
> *may* apply to the capabilities listed below. It does not — and
> cannot — establish whether any obligation has been satisfied.
>
> Before you ship, you must:
>
> 1. Have a qualified attorney admitted in each declared jurisdiction
>    review every item flagged `applies` or `may apply, verify`.
> 2. Read each cited primary source yourself (URLs are provided for
>    every applicability claim) — the agent may misread, and the
>    attorney's reading of the statute is what governs.
> 3. Treat any item not listed here as out of scope of *this* Skill,
>    not as out of scope of all law. The Skill covers
>    [JP / EU / US-CA / platform stores] only and does not handle
>    industry-specific regimes (healthcare, finance, broadcasting,
>    controlled substances, gambling, etc.).
>
> If this output was generated more than the declared re-verification
> cadence ago without a recorded re-verification pass, the cited
> statutes and policies may be out of date. Re-run the Skill before
> relying on it. (An automated staleness banner appears above this
> block once two consecutive cadences have been missed; this sentence
> is a separate prompt for the human reader to check the
> re-verification date regardless of automation.)
```

## Staleness warning (rendered conditionally)

When the Skill's last-known re-verification date (recorded in this
Skill's frontmatter or in the project's `compliance.yml`) is more
than **365 days** before the current evaluation date, the output's
Disclaimer section gains an additional banner *above* the standard
block:

```markdown
> ⚠ **STALENESS WARNING.** This Skill's jurisdiction reference files
> have not been re-verified for more than 12 months. Citations may be
> out of date. Run [`docs-researcher`'s research-domain re-verification
> protocol](../../skills/verification-layer/research/protocol.md) on
> each jurisdiction reference file before relying on this output.
```

The 365-day threshold is doubled from the 6-month re-verification
cadence because re-verification is *expected* every 6 months —
crossing 12 months means two consecutive cadence misses, which is the
operator-facing failure threshold defined in
[`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) §"Skill update cadence".

## Why the block cannot be customized

Operators may be tempted to soften the disclaimer ("this is for our
internal staging use only, the warning is overkill"). The disclaimer
exists precisely because the staging-only use case is the one most
likely to drift into production without re-evaluation. The block is
locked. The Override Protocol in [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) explicitly
disallows overriding it.
